Welcome to Hearthvale

You are the gentle spirit of a little round world. This early build lets you shape the land; villagers, your hand and your god pet arrive in the next phases.

  • Raise and Lower (2 3): click to lift land, right-click anywhere to sink it. Hold to keep going. Neighbouring points never differ by more than one step, so the land slides into gentle slopes.
  • Hand (1): drag the land to look around.
  • The world is a planet: travel far enough and you come back round. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, Q E to turn, WASD to pan, Space to pause. Right-drag or middle-drag also turns the view.
  • On a phone: drag to look, pinch to zoom, twist two fingers to turn.
