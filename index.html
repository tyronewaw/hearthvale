Welcome to Hearthvale

You are the gentle spirit of a little round world and its blue tribe. Shape the land, guide your people with your hand, and watch the village grow.

  • Raise and Lower (2 3): click to lift land, right-click anywhere to sink it, and hold to keep going. Villagers settle on flat land, and the more flat open land around a home, the bigger it grows: tepee, hut, round hut, big hut, longhouse, great lodge.
  • The Hand (1): grab villagers, trees and rocks and drop them somewhere new. Drop a villager on a building to give them that job; drop trees on the Hearth for wood. Drag empty land to look around.
  • Build (4): farms, lumber camps, fishing huts and shrines cost wood. Builders from the Hearth put them up faster.
  • Totem (5): wanderers head there to look for land.
  • The world is a planet: travel far enough and you come back round. Scroll to zoom, Q E or right-drag to turn, WASD to pan, Space to pause. Your world saves itself in this browser.
