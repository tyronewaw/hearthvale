Welcome to Hearthvale

You are the gentle spirit of a little round world and its blue tribe. Shape the land, guide your people with your hand, and watch the village grow.

  • Raise and Lower (2 3): click to lift land, right-click anywhere to sink it, and hold to keep going. Villagers settle on flat land, and the more flat open land around a home, the bigger it grows: tepee, hut, round hut, big hut, longhouse, great lodge.
  • The Hand (1): grab villagers, trees and rocks and drop them somewhere new. Drop a villager on a building to give them that job; drop trees on the Hearth for wood. Drag empty land to look around.
  • Build (4): farms, lumber camps, fishing huts and shrines cost wood and take a 2×2 patch of land; the preview snaps to the corner nearest your pointer. Builders from the Hearth put them up faster.
  • Totem (5): wanderers head there to look for land.
  • Your pet has its own needs and habits. Click it with the Hand to stroke it, right-click to scold. Stroke it straight after it does something you like and it will do that more; scold it after mischief and it will do it less. Drop trees on it as snacks. Use the leash: Free, Follow your hand, Stay, or Send it somewhere.
  • Blessings (6–0) unlock as your tribe grows: Bloom, Level, Rain, Rise and Festival. They cost faith, which your people (and priests) give you. Your pet learns tricks by watching you cast them.
  • The Red tribe lives on a far shore. Nobody fights: win them over with big homes and Shrines near them, festivals, blessings on their land and a kind pet. Their flags turn blue as they warm to you, and they hold festivals of their own when kindness counts double. The glowing lines on the ground are the borders of your land (blue) and theirs (red): carry Red wanderers into your land and they join you. B shows or hides the borders.
  • The world is a planet: travel far enough and you come back round. Scroll to zoom, Q E or right-drag to turn, WASD to pan, Space to pause. Your world saves itself in this browser.
