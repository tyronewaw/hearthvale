Welcome to Hearthvale

You are the gentle spirit of an island tribe. Shape the land, guide your people with your hand, and raise a god pet.

  • The Hand (1): grab villagers and trees, then drop them or let go mid-swing to throw. Drop a villager on a building to give them that job. Click your pet to stroke it, right-click to scold it. Drag empty land to look around.
  • Raise and Lower (2 3): click to lift land, right-click anywhere to sink it. Villagers settle on flat land, and the more flat open land around a home, the bigger it grows: tepee, hut, round hut, big hut, longhouse, great lodge.
  • Build (4): farms, lumber camps, fishing huts and shrines cost wood. Builders from the Hearth put them up faster.
  • Your pet has its own needs and habits. Stroke it straight after it does something you like and it will do that more; scold it after mischief and it will do it less. It also learns by watching your blessings. Use the leash: Free, Follow your hand, or Stay.
  • Your pet grows from baby to young to grand as you feed, stroke and work with it. Cast Bloom, Rain or a Festival near it twice and it learns to cast that trick itself.
  • Scroll to zoom, WASD or arrows to pan, Space to pause. On a phone: drag to look, pinch to zoom, tap your pet to stroke it and hold on it to scold. Your island saves itself in this browser.
